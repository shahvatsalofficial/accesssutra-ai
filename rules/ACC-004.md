# ACC-004: Human AT evidence

**Requirement:** Screen reader and cognitive findings require attributed real human observations.

**Applies to:** CLI, browser adapter, reports, agents, skills, server, integrations and public release messaging.

**Enforcement:** Code guard where feasible; otherwise mandatory human review with explicit `not-run` or `unknown` status.

**Test/evidence:** A review record must show the affected command, artifact or decision and whether enforcement was executed, observed or not verified.

**Violation:** Stop the unsafe operation, preserve a minimal audit event, and escalate to Privacy & Consent Sentinel / Independent Access Assurance Auditor.
