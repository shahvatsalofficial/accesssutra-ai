## How accurate is "100%"?

The corpus was written by the maintainer from HTML and ARIA semantics before looking at tool output, then used to check the tool. It is small and not independent. Treat 100% as "consistent with its own spec", not as a real-world accuracy figure. Independent labelled data is on the roadmap.
